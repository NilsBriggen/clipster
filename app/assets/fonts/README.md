# Fonts

`wine-tahoma.ttf` and `wine-tahoma-bold.ttf` are the Tahoma-compatible fonts made by the [Wine](https://www.winehq.org) project. They are not Microsoft's Tahoma. They come from Wine 11.21 (its `fonts/tahoma.ttf` and `fonts/tahomabd.ttf`) and are licensed under the **GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later** (see `LGPL-2.1.txt`). Clipster embeds them unchanged to get the classic 90s interface look on every system.
